# Engine fix A/B - classic25 benchmark (2026-08-12)

Same 25 papers, same translation caches; only the rendering engine differs.
Three evaluation points: `8952e12` (pre-fix baseline), `c29b53e`+`db0e1b7`
(first fix round), and the merged engine at `58f4401` (adds sibling-list
harmonization, caption readability, CJK font metrics, float wrapping and
pixel-verified table rules).

## What the fixes were

1. Multi-line paragraphs grow downward into free inter-block space instead
   of crushing their font (root cause: bilingual term glosses lengthen the
   Chinese text by roughly one wrapped line).
2. Inline formula sprite trims relocate to whitespace gaps in the rendered
   ink profile instead of span bbox edges (span boxes carry ascent headroom,
   so bbox-edge cuts sliced Sigma limits and subscripts).
3. Paragraphs starting with a citation after a split bold lead ("[28] is
   a ...") no longer classify as bibliography entries, which silently
   dropped them from translation and QA.

Biggest movers: gpt3 46->26 (code-font ratio + sample-box policy),
faster_rcnn 18->10 (bibliography wrap guards; references no longer
translated or crushed), mamba 29->25, sam 23->20.

What the fixes were:

1. Wrapped reference author lines ("... A. W. Smeul-", "... J. Malik,
   "Rich feature") no longer terminate the bibliography range.
2. Position-anchored table captions grow upward by their height deficit
   instead of crushing (faster_rcnn tables at 5.9-6.9pt -> body scale).
3. Chart pages stop counting as damaged tables: dashed gridlines are
   dropped and >=2 spanning vertical edges mark plot frames (adam, clip).
4. Monospace must dominate a line before it counts as code: GPT-3 prose
   citing P(completion|context) tokens translates again. Quoted sample
   floats stay verbatim by policy (inspector exempts algorithm regions
   and labeled example boxes).

Residual (by frequency): font_size_drift 92 (dense two-column bodies
boxed in by neighbours - needs cascade shifting), formula_clipped 24,
untranslated_english 24, text_overlap 22. The six new CC papers carry
their own long tail (cot 37, mamba 25, dpo 19).
