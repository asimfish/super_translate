Same 25 papers, same translation caches; only the rendering engine differs.

## What the fixes were

1. Multi-line paragraphs grow downward into free inter-block space instead
   of crushing their font (root cause: bilingual term glosses lengthen the
   Chinese text by roughly one wrapped line).
2. Inline formula sprite trims relocate to whitespace gaps in the rendered
   ink profile instead of span bbox edges (span boxes carry ascent headroom,
   so bbox-edge cuts sliced Sigma limits and subscripts).
3. Paragraphs starting with a citation after a split bold lead ("[28] is
   a ...") no longer classify as bibliography entries, which silently
   dropped them from translation and QA.
