The benchmark cache and gate now reject stale reports and translated PDFs.
This prevents an old artifact from being counted as a new-engine result, which
was the cause of inconsistent strict-pass totals when legacy reports were
re-evaluated with current QA. The next 43-paper aggregate must be generated by
a full content-addressed retranslation before it is eligible for publication.
Translation model, prompt/terminology policy, and the selected regular, bold,
fallback, and math font files are part of that artifact identity.
